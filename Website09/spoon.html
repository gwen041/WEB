Wooden Spoon

A wooden spoon is a versatile, essential kitchen tool used primarily for stirring, mixing, and tasting, especially with hot liquids, sauces, and delicate ingredients. Because wood is non-conductive, the handle stays cool to the touch, and it will not scratch non-stick, enamel, or copper cookware. They are durable, heat-resistant, and non-reactive, making them ideal for long cooking times.
