Bicol Express


Ingredients

  • Pork Belly
  • Coconut
    • Milk
    • Cream
  • Shrimp Paste
  • Garlic
  • Chili Pepper
  • Ginger
  • Onions
  • Green Peppers
  • Maggi Magic Sarap

How To Cook

  1. Combine the ingredients.
  2. Stir the ingredients until well distributed.
  3. Cover the wok and let the coconut milk boil.
  4. Simmer for 50 - 60 minutes.
  5. Add water as needed.
  6. Add the coconut cream.
  7. Season with magic sarap.
  8. Continue simmering until the sauce thickens.
  9. Transfer to a serving bowl. Serve with rice.
